## Usage Notes

### Image Optimization
- All images served as WebP via Next.js `<Image>` component
- Responsive sizes: 640, 750, 828, 1080, 1200, 1920
- Lazy loading for below-fold images
- Blur placeholder for progressive loading

### Brand Logo Treatment
- White variant on dark backgrounds
- Full color on light backgrounds
- Consistent sizing: 268x125 or 268x152

### Product Image Treatment
- Center-cropped for consistent framing
- Subtle vignette for cinematic feel
- Consistent aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3
