# Storyboard.md — 35 Cinematic Scenes

## The Film

**A person enters darkness. Light reveals materials. Materials reveal craft. Craft reveals home.**

This is not a website. It is a film that responds to scroll. The viewer is the camera operator. Every scroll advances the story. Every pause lets the moment breathe.

## Scroll Mechanics

### Progress
- Thin vertical line on right edge (2px, Ember)
- Expands to 4px on hover
- Shows percentage

### Scene Indicators
- Small dots on right edge, one per scene
- Current scene dot is Ember
- Click to jump to scene

### Speed
- Normal scroll: 1 scene per scroll
- Momentum: Can skip scenes
- Keyboard: Arrow keys move one scene
- Touch: Swipe gestures

### Performance
- Only current + adjacent scenes rendered
- Images lazy load (2 scenes away)
- Animations pause when not in viewport
- Target: 60fps all devices
